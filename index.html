Fixing Carpentry WA is a premium residential carpentry company servicing Perth. We work right across the build: new homes, renovations, decking, cladding, custom timber and the detailed internal finishing we are best known for.

What ties it all together is how we work. Everything set out properly, every join treated as though it has our name on it, and a finish you can run your hand across and feel.

We work alongside Perth's custom builders, architects, interior designers and homeowners on high-end residential projects, and we take real pride in the detail most people never notice.
